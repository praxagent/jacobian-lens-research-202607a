# Literature and audience landscape for the J-lens CKA atlas note (searched 2026-09-05)

All items below were located via public web search; no draft text or unpublished numbers were used in queries. Items I could not confirm are flagged **[unverified]**. Local-repo facts (what the draft already cites, commit dates) come from read-only greps.

## 1. Anthropic's paper, the J-lens, and what they claim about depth

- **Paper.** "Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models", Gurnee, Sofroniew, Pearce, ... Batson, Lindsey (16 authors). Transformer Circuits, **2026-07-06**: https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html. arXiv 2607.15495, **v1 2026-07-16**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15495. Landing page: https://www.anthropic.com/research/global-workspace. Models: Claude Sonnet 4.5 (primary), Haiku 4.5, Opus 4.5/4.6.
- **Depth claim.** "For each pair of layers, we compute the similarity of the J-space's geometry ... using centered kernel alignment (CKA), which compares, for each pair of layers, the matrices of pairwise similarities among J-lens vectors" (Fig. 27): "a clear block structure: an early block encompassing roughly the first third of the model, a long middle block, and a small late block." Boundaries (~L38 to ~L92 on Sonnet 4.5) are triangulated from four signatures in Fig. 28: next-token accuracy, excess kurtosis, position autocorrelation, effective dimensionality. Framing: "three functional regions — sensory (early), workspace (middle), and motor (late)".
- **Their own hedges (useful to quote against "stages").** "The Jacobian lens is an imperfect tool, which we believe only approximately and incompletely captures the model's underlying workspace structure"; early-layer emptiness may mean "the J-lens is degenerate at these depths" or that early layers carry no linearly accessible verbalizable content; "we do not provide evidence that non-J-space processing consists of clearly encapsulated modules." The paper reports **no null/random baseline for the CKA block structure** and **no corpus-composition ablation** (only "varying the number of contexts over which we average"). Fit: 1,000 prompts × 128 tokens from a pretraining-like corpus; README says ~100 prompts is usable.
- **Lens comparisons.** Logit lens and J-lens "agree closely in the model's last several layers and diverge earlier"; Gurnee on tuned lens: "too good at its job" (https://x.com/wesg52/status/2074186929705500690, via search snippet only).
- **Released.** `anthropics/jacobian-lens` (Apache-2.0; "reference implementation, not maintained"; fit/apply/visualize; synthetic prompt sets): https://github.com/anthropics/jacobian-lens. Partnership with Neuronpedia for open-model lenses (https://www.neuronpedia.org/jlens). External-commentary PDF: https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/files/4zrzovbb/website/cc4be2488d65e54a6ed06492f8968398ddc18ebe.pdf. The library README does not mention CKA at all; the CKA convention the note builds on is the paper's, not the code's.

## 2. Dehaene & Naccache and other cognitive-science / philosophical responses

- **Dehaene & Naccache**, "Does Claude possess a conscious global workspace?" (written May/June 2026, posted July 2026): https://unicog.org/wp_2025/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Dehaene-and-Naccache-Workspace-commentary-on-Gurnee-Lindsey-June-2026.pdf. Calls the result "a landmark in consciousness research." Directly relevant to the note's ignition-depth test: "Ignition remains to be fully demonstrated"; they propose presenting graded-strength stimuli and asking "whether J-space representations switch on with a threshold-like nonlinearity, while earlier, non-J-space layers rise monotonically with input strength," plus a bimodality test at threshold and dual-task interference. Also: "layer depth could mimic the temporal dynamics of the human workspace"; capacity ~25 "may be artificially elevated by the technique"; J-space is "a sparse subframe," not dedicated units; recurrence is absent. This is the closest published framing for treating band onset as an ignition analogue, and the note should cite it when it reports no verdict.
- **Butlin, Shiller, Plunkett, Long** (Eleos AI / Rethink Priorities), same PDF: separate three graded claims ("privileged set" < "privileged stream" < "GWT workspace") and warn that capacity estimates inherit "the acknowledged distortions of the J-lens."
- **Neel Nanda** (same PDF; and LessWrong review 2026-07-06, https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/zFJ3ZdQwrTWE9jT5S/a-review-of-anthropic-s-global-workspace-paper). His Qwen3.6-27B replication (25 Pile prompts, first four tokens skipped for high norm) says of CKA: "somewhat similar squares emerging, though less clean ... two or three somewhat overlapping bands (four or five bands total)." This is the only prior public observation that open-model lens CKA looks messier than Claude's; the note's zoo is the systematic answer and does not currently cite it (grep: 0 hits for "Nanda").
- **Gavin Leech**, Paradigm 3, 2026-07-07: "half of this looks like a selection effect, where the estimator chosen already entails the conclusion": https://www.paradigm3.org/research/jspace/.
- **David Louapre**, HF blog 2026-07-13, access-vs-phenomenal split, cautious: https://huggingface.co/blog/dlouapre/j-space.
- **Zvi Mowshowitz**, 2026-07-07, aggregates reactions (Robert Long, Janus, others): https://thezvi.substack.com/p/no-space-like-j-space.
- **Press**: MIT Technology Review 2026-07-09 and follow-up "what it does and doesn't show" 2026-07-13: https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/07/13/1140343/what-anthropics-latest-ai-discovery-does-and-doesnt-show/.
- **Rahbar, "The Ignition Index"**, arXiv 2608.05160 (abs page shows v1 **2026-05-26**, which conflicts with the 2608 identifier; **[unverified date, no affiliation shown]**): https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05160. Defines ignition as the steepness of a 4-parameter sigmoid fit to per-layer linear-probe accuracy vs input signal strength (masking/noise/corruption) on BLiMP/CoNLL/UD; 12 models (GPT-2, Pythia, Gemma-2 2B/9B, Huginn, Mamba). No J-lens, no CKA, no absolute ignition layers reported, and it lists "layer depth as a proxy for processing time is principled but imperfect" as a limitation. The note's ignition-depth design should be positioned against this (different operationalisation, same GWT motivation).

## 3. Community J-lens work since July 2026

- **Neuronpedia.** Blog "Welcome to the J-Space" (Lin & Chanin, 2026-07-10): 36 pre-fitted lenses at launch, explicit call for community fits: https://www.neuronpedia.org/blog/jacobian-lens. The draft's "36" should be pinned to a dated snapshot; the explorer below says 38.
- **eliebak / open-jlens-data** (Elie Bakouch; HF affiliation from general knowledge, **[unverified]**). Repo first commit **2026-07-11**, vendored jlens, runs E1..E11: https://github.com/eliebak/open-jlens-data. v1 CKA explorer (38 models + Inkling/Laguna guests; "4,096 token strings shared by all 38 tokenizers, each model reading them through its own unembedding"; 1,000 wikitext prompts; depth reindexed to 0–100%; "a bright 45° band" = same organisation at same relative depth; **no null control**): https://eliebak.com/viz/jspace-open. Tweet 2026-07-07: "the patterns are weirdly universal ... even between unrelated families like llama and olmo" (https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2074532904009421260, snippet only).
- **eliebak v2 explorer, the most important positioning item**: https://eliebak.com/viz/jspace-open-v2 (Wayback has a snapshot **2026-07-16**; I could not open the archive to confirm the snapshot's contents, so section-level dating is **[partly unverified]**; the repo's `runs/E5_datasets` folder with wikitext/code/math/finepdfs fits for qwen3-1.7b, qwen3-4b, gemma3-1b-pt, delphi-3.4b is in the **2026-07-11** commit). Live text: "We refit the lens on Python code, web math, and PDF-extracted prose for four models"; "The same qwen3-4b band measures 594 directions through code and 202 through PDF prose"; "A dictionary size is a property of a (model, corpus) pair, not of a model"; "The input-side layers are rewritten wholesale"; "Code ... makes two models look least alike." Also training-emergence: "The blocks emerge with no supervision, and their edges reappear in every experiment"; "The geometric boundary starts at ρ = 0.46 and migrates onto the cliff over the next six trillion tokens"; cross-model "CKA 0.5–0.7 at matched depth"; MoE section; transplant (rotation-only transfer, 94–96%). The note's `corpus_dependence/PREREG.md` was committed **2026-07-25** and neither it, `results.md`, nor the draft cites eliebak's corpus or emergence work (draft cites only the v1 explorer and the repo). This is prior public work on the same question and must be cited and differentiated (his metric is dictionary size / CKA per corpus; the note's is band-boundary movement).
- **PrimeIntellect.** `inkling-jlens` (2026-07-17; ~950B MoE, 66 layers, 250 wikitext-103 prompts, NVFP4; ships 65×65 CKA npz + heatmap + split-half same-layer CKA median 0.997; Apache-2.0): https://huggingface.co/PrimeIntellect/inkling-jlens. `Laguna-XS.2-jlens` (2026-07-17; 33B; 200 prompts; probe "W_U[ids]·γ ... 4096 frozen vocabulary indices"; NVFP4 1−CKA≈0.008 vs version drift median same-layer CKA 0.638; credits open-jlens-data): https://huggingface.co/PrimeIntellect/Laguna-XS.2-jlens. - **Other replications/tools.** Bhatia, Blank, Nanda meta-tokens (LW 2026-07-20, Qwen3.6-27B, uses CKA): https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/6ek6n7yZ5DzfarJHy/. Yan et al. - **Not found** (searched; absence is not proof): any third-party layer×layer lens-CKA study with a random/null control; any own- vs shared-vocabulary CKA comparison; any causal test of whether lens CKA bands are computational stages beyond Anthropic's ablations and Nanda's qualitative remark. ## 4. Adjacent literature to cite or position against

**CKA validity (the note's method core).** Kornblith et al. 2019 (ICML; already cited). Nguyen, Raghu, Kornblith 2021, block structure in CKA of wide/deep nets (ICLR; https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.15327) and 2022 "On the Origins of the Block Structure Phenomenon" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07184): blocks arise from a dominant first PC / dominant datapoints, seed-dependent. This is the canonical prior for "a CKA block is a readout/dominant-direction property, not a stage" and for the Gemma flatness story. Davari et al. 2023, "Reliability of CKA" (ICLR; https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.16156): CKA is manipulable by translating a subset of points, sensitive to outliers. Ding, Denain, Steinhardt 2021 (NeurIPS; https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.01661): sensitivity/specificity tests for similarity measures. Cui et al. 2022 "Deconfounded Representation Similarity" (NeurIPS; https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.00095): CKA is confounded by population structure of the item set, which is the formal version of the note's probe-vocabulary dependence. Williams et al. 2021 shape metrics (NeurIPS; https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.14739). Klabunde et al. 2025 survey (ACM CSUR; https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06329). Wu et al. 2020, CKA across BERT layers (ACL; https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.01172). None of these are cited in the draft (grep: 0 hits for Davari, Nguyen, Williams, Ding, Klabunde).

**Depth structure / lenses.** nostalgebraist 2020 logit lens (https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/AcKRB8wDpdaN6v6ru/). Belrose et al. 2023 tuned lens (https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08112): per-layer translator transfer penalties correlate ρ=−0.78 with covariance similarity, "rogue dimensions" cause asymmetry, and base-model translators transfer to fine-tuned models (relevant to the instruct-vs-base section). Lad, Gurnee, Tegmark 2024 "Stages of Inference?" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19384; appears cited). Csordás et al. 2025 (NeurIPS; https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13898): second half of layers contributes less, with a phase transition. Skean et al. ICML 2025 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02013). Sun et al. "Transformer Layers as Painters" (AAAI 2025; https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09298). Gromov et al. 2024 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17887). Liu et al. "Inverse Depth Scaling From Most Layers Being Similar" (ICML 2026; https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05970). Wolfram & Schein 2025, "Layers at Similar Depths Generate Similar Activations Across LLM Architectures" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08775): the activation-space precedent for eliebak's 45° universality; the note's cross-model section should cite it. Huh et al. 2024 Platonic Representation Hypothesis (ICML; https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07987) and Jha et al. 2025 vec2vec (NeurIPS; https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12540) for cross-model geometry convergence. Bhattacharya & Kolli 2026 (arXiv 2607.18348, 2026-07-20): displacement "larger early and late, with a quieter middle third," Procrustes decomposition, explicitly descriptive. Szablewski & Masiak 2025 "Activation Transport Operators" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17540): linear layer-to-layer transport maps and "transport efficiency", a close methodological cousin of the J-lens transport. Wendler et al. 2024 "Do Llamas Work in English?" (ACL; https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10588) for vocabulary/script effects in lens readouts (the note's script-family regression). Draft grep: 0 hits for "logit lens", "tuned lens", Belrose, Wolfram, Csordás, Heimersheim.

**Norm growth.** Heimersheim & Turner 2023, residual norms grow ~1.045×/layer in GPT-2-XL (https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/8mizBCm3dyc432nK8/). Linear CKA is per-layer scale-invariant, but heavy-tailed per-token norms (attention-sink first tokens; Nanda dropped four) dominate Gram matrices, which is the Nguyen/Davari mechanism. The note should say what it does about high-norm positions when fitting J.

**Identifiability.** Zheng, Ng, Zhang 2022 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.07751) and Hyvärinen et al. 2023 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00866) are in background.md; Roeder et al. 2021 linear identifiability (https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.00810); Paulo & Belrose, SAEs on the same data learn different features (ICLR 2026; https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16615); Nelson, Karaletsos, Locatello, identifiable SAEs (ICML 2026; https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31245); Zheng et al., Diverse Dictionary Learning (ICLR 2026; https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17568). These connect only if the note claims lens directions are identified sources; the equal-norm perturbation result is an instrument-validity claim, so one framing sentence suffices.

## 5. Realistic audience and what would make the note notable

- **The J-lens ecosystem (primary, maybe 50–200 people who will actually open it):** Anthropic authors; Nanda's team (their public "less clean" CKA is the hook the zoo answers); Neuronpedia maintainers (Lin, Chanin) who host the 36+ lenses whose maps this note characterises; eliebak; PrimeIntellect; Phillips; the Alibaba/USTC and Gong & Wang groups. Notable to them: the first public 397B CKA map **with a null control** (nobody else ships one), the Gemma own-vs-shared-vocabulary artefact (it affects how Neuronpedia's Gemma lenses and eliebak's cross-model tables are read), the negative causal results (five nulls; the ecosystem has none published), and the converged fit-budget sweep (PrimeIntellect's split-half is the only comparable check).
- **Representational-similarity methodologists (Kornblith, Davari, Williams, Steinhardt, Klabunde circles):** the "bands are a readout property, 4/4 cells" result is a clean LLM instance of the block-structure literature and would be notable if framed in their vocabulary (dominant-direction / population-structure confound) rather than J-space vocabulary.
- **Cognitive scientists (Dehaene/Naccache, Butlin/Long):** the ignition-depth no-verdict and the "not computational stages" position bear on the "layer depth mimics time" hedge, but 4 of 6 models excluded is too weak to travel; frame as a pre-registered null design they could rerun with graded stimuli.
- **Press/generalists:** not a 20k-word audience; the July press cycle is over.
- **What hurts reach:** length (this crowd's reference reviews, Nanda's and Zvi's, are a fraction of the size); being ~two weeks behind eliebak's public corpus-dependence and training-emergence results without citing them; the 36 vs 38 lens count; missing the standard CKA-critique citations, which is the first thing a similarity-metrics reader will check. A 1,500-word "findings and figures" companion plus this note as the reference document would fit how the ecosystem actually reads.
